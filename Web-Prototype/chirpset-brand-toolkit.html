01 — Logo System
The Chirpset Mark

A robotic chirping bird — part circuit board, part creature. The antenna LED blinks green when scanning; the beak opens in a chirp when a device is detected. Circuit traces on the body hint at the hardware domain.

Clear space

Usage guidelines

✓ Do
Use approved mark variations at any size above 16px.
✗ Don't
Don't rotate, skew, or distort the mark.
✗ Don't
Don't apply arbitrary colors. Use the palette or mono version.
✗ Don't
No drop shadows, outlines, or effects on the mark.
