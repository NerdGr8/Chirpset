01 — Logo System
The Symmetric Mark

A front-facing robotic bird — perfectly bilateral. Two watchful eyes, a centered diamond beak, mirrored angular wings, and dual sound-wave arcs. The antenna LED pulses green when scanning. Circuit-board traces on the body root it in hardware.

Usage guidelines

✓ Do
Use approved mark variations at any size above 16 px.
✗ Don't
Don't rotate, skew, or distort the mark.
✗ Don't
Don't apply arbitrary colors outside the palette.
✗ Don't
No drop shadows, outlines, or effects.
